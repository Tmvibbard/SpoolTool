2 · Columns

What it found

Only two matter: something with a length in it, and something that says which runs are the same cable. Change anything it got wrong.

What makes two runs the same cable?

Runs that agree on every column you pick here get spooled together. On a job with no designation scheme, pick wire size and ground size instead.

4 · Cables

Reels and parallels

One row per cable found in the takeoff. Reel length is the put-up you can actually buy it on — 1000' on small wire, 500 or 250 or 200 on the big stuff. Colors are free text and only used to group the printout.

5 · Terminations

Every value found in the start and end location columns, listed once. A pull gets its start allowance plus its end allowance. Set the default, apply keyword rules, then fix the odd ones.
